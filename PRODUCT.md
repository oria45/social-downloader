# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single user (the owner) running this on their own computer (Mac or Windows). No public audience, no onboarding funnel, no multi-tenant concerns. Used ad hoc whenever they want to save a video, reel, or clip they found on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube, straight to local disk.

## Product Purpose

A local, single-screen utility: paste a link, see what's actually available to download (real title, thumbnail, resolutions, audio bitrate options pulled from the source), pick a quality, download. Success is zero friction and zero ambiguity, not conversion or engagement.

## Brand Personality

Warm, friendly, approachable. Three words: חם / ידידותי / נגיש (warm / friendly / accessible). The opposite of a cold, clinical admin panel.

## Anti-references

Explicitly not: generic SaaS-template look. The current build (light gray page background, plain white card, indigo/violet accent, flat rounded corners) reads as interchangeable with any AI-generated dashboard and should be moved away from, not iterated on.

## Design Principles

- Personal tool, not enterprise dashboard: no trust badges, no performative "professional" chrome. It answers to one person.
- Warmth over sterility: color and type choices should feel inviting, not corporate-neutral.
- One clear action at a time: the existing paste-then-pick-quality-then-download flow is correct; the redesign should sharpen that progression visually, not add steps or chrome around it.
- Approachable over polished-for-polish's-sake: friendly, plain-language copy and inviting shapes over generic minimalism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard: WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation. No additional accommodations specified.
